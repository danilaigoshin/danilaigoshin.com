— About

A short note from me.

I'm Danila — a senior frontend developer from Penza, Russia, with six years building React, TypeScript, and Next.js products. I started with a solo B2B e-commerce platform and later moved into larger product teams, where my focus became performance, architecture, migrations, and developer experience.

I tend to be the person on a team who cares about build times, performance budgets, and TypeScript adoption. I've migrated Webpack to Vite (ten times faster builds), brought a shopping cart from sluggish to instant, rewritten authentication without breaking anyone's session, and led a full TypeScript migration of a non-trivial codebase in a year.

I mentor others, host internal tech talks (state vs. cache, SSR vs. ISR, the usual), and use LLM tools pragmatically — for exploration, refactoring, documentation, and review support — while keeping engineering judgment and code ownership with the developer. I led my last team's adoption of Claude and Cursor.

I'm open to senior frontend roles, remote or relocation, with teams working on complex React products, performance, architecture, and frontend platform work. Ideally with a team that values quality, ownership, and long-term maintainability.

Yours, D. Igoshin
